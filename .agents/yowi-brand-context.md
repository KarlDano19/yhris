# YOWI Brand Context
*YAHSHUA Outsourcing Worldwide, Inc.*
*Last updated: 2026-09-10*

## Company Identity
**Full Name:** YAHSHUA Outsourcing Worldwide, Inc. (YOWI)
**Value Proposition:** "More for less."
**Vision:** "YAHSHUA Solutions, a better way to life."
**Mission:** "Make YAHSHUA Solutions accessible."
**Brand Character:** Faith-based, trustworthy, values-driven back-office partner.
**HQ:** Cagayan de Oro City, Philippines

## Track Record
- 17+ years in operation
- 100,000+ users nationwide
- Micro to Large enterprises served

## Services
1. YAHSHUA Payroll Online
2. YAHSHUA Books Online (Accounting)
3. YAHSHUA Tax Online (EOPT-ready)
4. YAHSHUA HRIS
5. YAHSHUA DOLO Training App

## Key Differentiators
- SOC2 Type 2 Certified
- ISO 27001 Certified
- GDPR Compliant
- DPA 2012 & NPC Registered
- EOPT-Ready (BIR compliant)
- Proprietary software suite + managed services (not just outsourcing)

## Target Market
- All business sizes (Micro to Large)
- Philippines-wide
- Primary buyer: Business owners, Finance/HR heads needing affordable back-office infrastructure
- 20 industries: Manufacturing, Retail, Hospitality, Healthcare, Agriculture, BPO, Education, and more

## Notable Clients
Del Monte Philippines, DTI Region X, University of St. La Salle - Bacolod, Polymedic Medical Plaza, Mindanao Golden Grains, 1A Express Hotel, Makimura Ramen, Engagis Inc., and others.

## Partners
Globe Business, RCBC, Sterling Bank of Asia, CyTech International

## Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Trustworthy, professional, accessible
- **Personality:** Reliable, faith-inspired, results-oriented, people-first
- **Use:** Outcome language, compliance confidence, relatable pain points
- **Avoid:** Generic outsourcing clichés, cold corporate language, em dashes

## Core Messaging Pillars
1. Trusted & Proven (17+ yrs, 100k+ users, enterprise clients)
2. Enterprise-Grade Security (SOC2, ISO 27001, GDPR)
3. All-in-One Back Office (Payroll + Accounting + Tax + HR + Training)
4. Compliant by Design (EOPT-ready, always BIR-updated)
5. More for Less (back-office team quality at outsourcing cost)

## Strategic Notes
- Lead gen should drive to consultation/demo (no public pricing on YOWI corporate site; YAHSHUA HRIS itself does publish pricing)
- Compliance certifications are the strongest differentiator — lean into these
- "Worldwide" positioning is aspirational; focus messaging on PH market for now
- No existing testimonials/case studies — flag need when producing trust-based content
- This blog serves yahshuahris.com (the HRIS product site) specifically — CTAs point to YAHSHUA HRIS demo booking, not generic YOWI contact
